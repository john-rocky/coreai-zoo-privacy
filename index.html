CoreAI Zoo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

CoreAI Zoo collects nothing. The apps (iOS and macOS) have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. All AI runs entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The apps contain no analytics or advertising SDKs and no user accounts. Your prompts, conversations, images, and any other input are never collected, stored off-device, or transmitted to us or anyone else.

On-device processing

Every model runs locally on your device. Your inputs and the model's outputs stay on the device and are not sent anywhere.

Network use

The only network connection the apps make is to download the model files you explicitly choose, hosted on Hugging Face (huggingface.co). These are public file downloads and contain none of your personal information. As the file host, Hugging Face may log standard request metadata such as your IP address; see the Hugging Face Privacy Policy. The apps make no other network requests.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties — there is no data to share.

Children

The apps are not directed at children and collect no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
